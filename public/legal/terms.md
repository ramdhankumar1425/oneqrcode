## The service

oneqrcode lets you create QR codes and manage where they point. **Dynamic codes** encode a permanent short link that redirects through our servers, so you can change the destination at any time without reprinting. **Static codes** encode a destination directly and cannot be changed after they are generated. Static codes are unlimited on every plan.
